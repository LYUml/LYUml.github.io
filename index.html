A personal detour

Figuring out what wasn’t for me.

Before my university entrance exams, I had no idea what I wanted to do next. So I skipped classes for a bit and tried an internship at Tencent. It had little to do with what I study or work on now. I just wanted to see what that world was like.

I came away sure of one thing: finance wasn’t for me. Honestly, I couldn’t stand the polished self-interest I felt around me. Even eating an apple seemed to get dressed up as a marker of elite taste, and the LinkedIn personas of some colleagues made that feeling even harder to shake. I found the whole performance deeply uncomfortable. It was an experiment, and figuring out what I didn’t want was a useful result.
